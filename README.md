# NFC KeyVault - Android Card Emulation (HCE) Project

This is a complete, native Android Studio project configured for **Host-based Card Emulation (HCE)**.

## How to Run on your Android Phone:
1. Open this folder in **Android Studio** (Koala, Iguana, Hedgehog or newer).
2. Connect your Android phone with **USB Debugging** enabled (Developer Options).
3. Ensure **NFC is enabled** in your phone's Android Settings.
4. Click **Run 'app'** (Green play button) to install on your phone.
5. In Android Settings -> Connected Devices -> Connection Preferences -> NFC:
   - Ensure NFC is ON.
   - Under "Contactless Payments" or "Default Payment App", you can select NFC KeyVault if prompted.
6. Hold the back of your phone against your office door reader or turnstile.
